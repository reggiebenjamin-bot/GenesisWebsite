Protecting people

Care comes with rules. Here they are, in plain words.

Every rule is built into the screens, not added later. Each one links to the place you can see it working.

Always in a crisis reply

  • 988Call or text the Suicide and Crisis Lifeline
  • 911Call for an emergency

If someone writes in crisis, Hope does not improvise. It shows a reply written by people, and the pastor on call is alerted with no private words.

  1. 01

    Prayer stays with your pastors and prayer team.

    Only they can open a prayer request. Each one shows a lock and the names of who can see it.

    See It in Care ↗
  2. 02

    Alert emails carry no private words.From Shepherd

    A new request sends an email that says only "New request. Sign in to read." A member's reply notice says only "You have a reply." On the Care plan, prayer forms go by email only to the pastors' address.

    See an Alert ↗
  3. 03

    A record of who opened what.From Shepherd

    Every open, reply, change and archive is recorded, and nobody can edit it. Role changes are emailed to the lead pastor. Plus adds the Access Record screen to search and export it.

    See the Access Record ↗
  4. 04

    Archive, never delete.

    Prayer, threads and pastor notes are archived, so nothing is lost by accident. A person can still ask for a copy or for removal.

    See Archived Care ↗
  5. 05

    Member accounts are for adults.

    Each person confirms they are 18 or older when they first sign in, with a code sent by email. Members only ever see their own things.

    See the Sign In ↗
  6. 06

    The gifts picture protects small numbers.

    The church chart stays hidden until 25 people finish. Small counts show as "fewer than 5." Names appear only for people who said yes.

    See the Gifts Picture ↗
  7. 07

    A gifts score is plain arithmetic.

    The same answers always give the same result. Hope never sets or changes a score.

    See a Result ↗
  8. 08

    A person approves every message.

    Hope drafts welcome notes and everyday replies, labels them and waits. It never sends on its own, and it never drafts a reply to prayer or a personal message.

    See Drafts Waiting ↗
  9. 09

    Guests hear from you by email first.

    Guest follow up goes by email. Texting stays off unless the church turns it on.

    See a Guest ↗
