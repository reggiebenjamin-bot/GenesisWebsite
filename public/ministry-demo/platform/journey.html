Chapter three. What Hope does

It drafts, summarizes and sorts. Then it waits for a person.

Hope never sends on its own and never sets a gifts score. Crisis words always get the same reply, written by people, with 988 and 911.

See it

A Welcome Draft ↗ Drafts Waiting ↗ Care Inbox ↗ Handled With Care ↗ A Gifts Result ↗
Read the flow as a list
  1. Comes in: a Plan a Visit form, a Hope chat on the website, an everyday message, a prayer request, or answers to the gifts conversation.
  2. Hope may draft welcome notes and everyday replies, summarize a conversation or the week's care, and sort what has waited longest and what is due.
  3. Then it waits. A person decides: Approve and Send, Edit, or Not Now. Nothing goes out on its own.
  4. Goes out: sent by a person, by email first. Texting only if the church turns it on.
  5. Prayer is only sorted. A person writes every word of a reply to prayer or a personal message.
  6. Gifts answers are counted with plain arithmetic, not AI. The same answers always give the same score, and the member sees gifts to explore, never a verdict.
  7. Hope never sets a gifts score, never sends on its own, never drafts a reply to prayer or a personal message, and never counsels or improvises in a crisis.
  8. Crisis words, anywhere someone types, get a fixed reply written by people with 988 and 911 right away, and the pastor on call is alerted with no private words.
