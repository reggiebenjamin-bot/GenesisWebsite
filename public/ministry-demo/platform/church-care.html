If someone under 18 writes in

Member accounts are for adults. When a young person reaches the care inbox anyway, staff follow the same steps every time.

  1. Stop the private thread. Do not continue a one-to-one conversation with a minor.
  2. Bring in a parent or guardian, and tell the young person you are doing it.
  3. Follow your church's child protection policy, and report when the law requires it.
  4. Close the account kindly, and archive the thread. Nothing is deleted.
