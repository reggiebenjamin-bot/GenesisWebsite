## Notes for whoever swaps these out

- Grade: warm whites, a touch of extra yellow, natural skin. No cold blue or heavy black and white.
- Adults are the subject. Kids appear only in groups or from behind, never as a single-child close-up. The boy in the hero photo stands with the adults at the edge of the group.
- No readable logos, brand names or other church names. The pastor photo has a party balloon and a cake stand in the background, and the worship photo has a blank screen at the top that every crop leaves out.
- No photo here is shared with the other three Genesis Ministry templates, and none comes from the same shoot as theirs. The list of every photo in use is in templates/PHOTO-CLAIMS.txt.
- Where the same photo appears twice on the home page it is only in the looping photo strip, which repeats its set for a seamless scroll.
