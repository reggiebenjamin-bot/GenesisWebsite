# Goldenrod Commons Church: social marketing plan

**Look:** Warm Community (Genesis Ministry template)
**Church:** Goldenrod Commons Church, 3120 Wrenfield Ave, Des Moines, Iowa (Beaverdale). Sundays 9:00 and 10:45 AM.
**Plan length:** six months, starting Monday, November 30, 2026
**Team it is built for:** two people. A communications lead (staff or a committed volunteer, about 5 hours a week) and a Sunday volunteer who takes the photos and cuts the two weekly clips (about 3 hours a week). The pastors give 15 minutes every Thursday and record one short video a month. The hour-by-hour split is in section 5.

> Template preview: Goldenrod Commons is a fictional church. Swap in a real church's name, times, people and numbers before using this plan.

## 1. Who we are trying to reach

We are a neighborhood church, so we post for the people who live within about 15 minutes of Wrenfield Ave: Beaverdale, Waveland Park, Drake, Merle Hay, Johnston and Urbandale.

| Who | What their week looks like | What they need from us | Where they are |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Young families (28 to 45)**, often new to the neighborhood | Work, daycare pickup, soccer on Saturdays, scrolling during nap time | Friends for their family, a safe and fun place for kids, a clear "what happens on Sunday" | Instagram, Facebook groups, Google search |
| **Longtime neighbors and empty nesters (50 to 70)** | Volunteering, grandkids, neighborhood association, local news | Ways to serve, people to sit with, a church that cares about the block | Facebook, email, Nextdoor |
| **The curious and the drifted (22 to 40)** | Grew up around church, left, still wondering | Honest teaching, no pressure, proof that real people go here | YouTube, Instagram Reels |

**One sentence to keep in mind:** if a tired parent in Beaverdale saw only this post, would they know when we meet, where to park and that they would be welcome?

## 2. Three goals, with numbers

Measure from a starting count taken on November 30, 2026. Review every month (see section 11).

1. **40 planned visits from social in six months.** Count Plan Your Visit form submissions that arrive through links tagged for social media. That is about 7 a month.
2. **Grow the Porch Letter from 300 to 650 subscribers by May 31, 2027.** Every Thursday invite and every story link points to the newsletter sign-up as the soft next step.
3. **Grow local Instagram followers from 400 to 1,000 by May 31, 2027,** with at least 60 percent of followers in the Des Moines metro (Instagram Insights, top locations).

## 3. Platform mix and why

| Platform | Role | How often | Why it fits |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Instagram** | Main home. Invites, Reels, carousels, Sunday stories. | 4 to 5 feed posts a week, stories every Sunday and Saturday | Young families in Des Moines live here. Stories give us polls, questions and link stickers to the visit page. |
| **Facebook** | Events, longer posts, local groups. | 3 to 4 posts a week (cross-posted), every gathering as a Facebook Event | Our 50 plus neighbors are here, and Events get shared into neighborhood groups. |
| **YouTube** | Full messages and Shorts. | Full message every Sunday afternoon, 2 Shorts a week | People search YouTube before they visit. Series playlists let someone binge a whole series. |
| **Google Business Profile** | The front door for "church near me." | 1 post and 5 new photos a week | Most first-time guests find a church on Google Maps. Fresh photos and posts help us show up. |
| **Nextdoor** (optional) | Neighborhood events only. | 2 posts a month | Good for the food drive, porch nights and Christmas Eve. Not for sermon content. |
| **TikTok** | **Not yet.** Claim the handle, post nothing for six months. | None | Our core audience is families and neighbors over 28, and a small team does better on fewer channels. Revisit in June 2027 if the young adults group grows and a volunteer wants to own it. Our Reels can be reposted there with no extra work. |

## 4. Five content pillars

Aim for a mix across the week. No pillar should go two weeks without a post.

| Pillar | What it is | Share of posts | Examples |

| **1. Sundays at Goldenrod** | Invites and "what to expect" | 25% | This Sunday post, parking video, kids check-in walkthrough, story poll for which service |
| **2. The Message** | Teaching, turned into small pieces | 25% | Quote cards, 45 second clips, "three things from Sunday" carousels, group questions |
| **3. Neighbors and Stories** | Real people from the church family | 20% | Baptism stories, a volunteer spotlight, "how I found Goldenrod," pastor Q and A |
| **4. Love the Block** | Serving Beaverdale and Des Moines | 15% | Food drive, Porch Project updates, a shout-out to a local business or school |
| **5. Porch Life** | Light, warm, behind the scenes | 15% | Coffee bar crew at 8:15, the chili cook-off, sunflowers on the welcome table, the pastors' beagle |

### On a hard week, do only these three things

1. Thursday "This Sunday" invite with the time, address and the visit link.
2. Sunday message on YouTube.
3. The Porch Letter.

Everything else can wait a week. A quiet week never hurts a church. A wrong service time does.

## 6. The Sunday content workflow: one message, one week of posts

**Thursday before:** the preaching pastor sends the communications lead the big idea in one sentence, three lines that could stand alone, the main scripture, and one question for small groups.

**Sunday morning:**
- Volunteer photographer follows the shot list (section 9) and uploads 20 to 30 selects to the shared Drive folder by 6 PM.
- Communications lead posts live stories using the three vertical videos the volunteer shoots (welcome, worship, a laugh in the lobby).

**Sunday afternoon:**
- Upload the full message to YouTube by 4 PM. Title format: *Message title | Series name, part number | Goldenrod Commons*. Add chapters and a link to Plan Your Visit in the first line of the description.
- Thumbnail from the Canva template: one photo, the title in Fredoka, the Sunflower bar.

**One message becomes:**

| Piece | Made from | Where it goes | Day |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Quote card** | The strongest of the three lines | Instagram feed, Facebook, Google Business Profile | Monday |
| **Clip 1** (45 to 60 sec) | The heart of the message, captions burned in | Reels, Facebook Reels, YouTube Shorts | Tuesday |
| **Carousel** (5 slides) | "Three things from Sunday" or the group questions | Instagram, Facebook | Wednesday |
| **Email** | Message link, one question to think about, events, a prayer | Porch Letter | Thursday |
| **Clip 2** (20 to 40 sec) | A story or funny moment from the message | Reels and Shorts | Friday |
| **Story poll** | "Which service works for you?" plus a question sticker: "What can we pray about for you?" | Instagram and Facebook stories | Saturday |

**Clip editing in 30 minutes:** drop the YouTube file into CapCut or Descript, find the two moments the pastor flagged, crop to 9:16, add auto captions in Nunito Sans, add the Goldenrod end card (logo and "Sundays 9:00 and 10:45"), export.

## 8. Ten sample captions

Write like a neighbor talking over the fence. Short lines. One clear ask. No emojis.

**1. This Sunday invite (Instagram, Thursday)**
> Pull up a chair this Sunday. We meet at 9:00 and 10:45 at 3120 Wrenfield Ave in Beaverdale. Coffee is on at 8:30, kids have their own rooms, and jeans are great. First time? Tap the link in our bio and tell us you are coming. Look for the yellow umbrellas out front. A greeter will be right there.
> #Beaverdale #DesMoines #DSMchurch

**2. Quote card (Monday)**
> "Love your neighbor is not a metaphor. It has an address." Pastor Marcus, from Casseroles and courage. Who is the neighbor you are thinking about right now? Watch the full message on our YouTube channel, link in bio.

**3. Message clip (Tuesday)**
> Peace is not the absence of noise. Pastor Ellie on what it looks like to be steady when the week is anything but. Watch the full message from Sunday on YouTube, and send this to someone who could use a quiet minute today.

**4. Volunteer spotlight (Wednesday)**
> Meet Rosa. She has run our coffee bar for six years and knows exactly how everyone takes it. "I just like being the first friendly face," she says. Want to join the coffee crew? We serve once a month and we will teach you the espresso machine. Reply here or tap Serve Teams in our bio.

**5. Kids ministry (Friday)**
> Wondering what your kids will do on Sunday? Here is the short version: check-in opens at 8:45 and 10:30, every room has trained and background-checked leaders, and there is a lot of glue stick. Swipe for the full walkthrough, then plan your visit with the link in bio.

**6. Serve Day (Facebook Event)**
> Saturday, December 12, 9 AM to noon: Coats for Beaverdale. We are collecting and sorting warm coats for families at three neighborhood schools. Bring a clean coat, bring a friend, and stay for hot chocolate on the porch. All ages welcome. Tap Going so we know how many cups to fill.

**7. Christmas Eve announcement**
> Christmas Eve at Goldenrod Commons: candlelight services at 4:00 and 6:00 PM. About an hour, family friendly, and yes, everyone gets a candle. If you have been meaning to try church again, this is a gentle night to do it. Bring someone with you. Details and parking at the link in bio.
> #DesMoinesChristmas #DSMfamilies #Beaverdale

**8. Story poll (Saturday)**
> Come say hi tomorrow. Which service works for you? 9:00 or 10:45. Tap to vote, then tap the link to plan your visit.

**9. Baptism story (Wednesday)**
> Last Sunday, Tasha went under the water and came up grinning. "I spent years thinking I had to get my life together first. I didn't." Thinking about baptism yourself? We would love to talk with you. Send us a message or tap Ask About Baptism on our website.

**10. Porch Letter push (Thursday)**
> Every Thursday morning we send one short email: what is happening Sunday, a prayer for the week, and the newest message. It takes five minutes to read and we never sell your email. Sign up for the Porch Letter with the link in our bio.

## 13. Simple guardrails

- **Kids:** no names, no single-child close-ups, and no photos of a child whose family has opted out.
- **Prayer requests:** never answer one in public comments. Move it to a private message and pass it to the prayer team.
- **Tone:** if it would sound strange said across a fence, do not post it.
- **Every post answers one of three questions:** when, where, or what next.
