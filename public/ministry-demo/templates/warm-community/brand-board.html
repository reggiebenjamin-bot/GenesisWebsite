04

Photography

Real people, warm light, shared tables

Photos should feel like a friend took them on a good Sunday. Warm daylight or lamplight, people mid-laugh or mid-sentence, and plenty of coffee mugs. Every photo sits in a rounded frame, often with a white snapshot border and a handwritten caption.

Do

  • Catch people talking, laughing or listening, not posing.
  • Shoot in warm daylight, golden hour, lamps or string lights.
  • Keep adults as the subject. Kids in groups or from behind.
  • Look for tables, mugs, porches, yards and open doors.
  • Leave a little calm space so a sticker or headline can sit nearby.

Avoid

  • Concert haze, colored stage lights or crowds as the lead photo.
  • Rows of people smiling at the camera like a staff photo.
  • Single-child close-ups or any readable name tag on a child.
  • Cold blue grades, heavy black and white, or harsh filters.
  • Logos, brand names or another church's name in the frame.

Grade: warm whites, a touch of extra yellow, natural skin.Frame: 28 to 34 px corners, one swoopy corner per page.Motion: slow drift on the hero photo, a gentle tilt on snapshots.

Motion character: neighborly bounce

  • Scroll inColumns, cards and rows rise one by one with a soft spring overshoot.
  • Wave lineA hand-drawn wave draws across each column, then settles to a short mark.
  • Hover panelA Porch White panel wipes up behind the column. Cards lift and tilt a degree.
  • SheenSunflower buttons catch the light once when they arrive and follow the cursor.
  • Changing wordThe hero word drops in after the last one leaves. They never overlap.
  • PulseThe announcement play button breathes gently. Everything stops for reduced motion.

Voice and tone

We sound like the neighbor who waves back

Warm, plain and a little playful. Short sentences. No jargon, no church-speak, no em dashes and no emojis. We say what time, where to park and what happens next.

House rules for every post

  • SizesSquare 1080 px. Story 1080 by 1920, words in the middle.
  • WordsTwelve or fewer on the graphic. The rest goes in the caption.
  • ColorOne Sunflower or Sky panel. Cocoa text, never yellow text.
  • MarkThe small sun mark in one corner.
  • PhotosAdults, warm light. No child alone, no name tags.
  • AskEvery post answers when, where or what next.
