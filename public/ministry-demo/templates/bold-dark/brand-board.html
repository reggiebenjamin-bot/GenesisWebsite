04

Photography

Shoot it like a live record, not a catalog. Warm stage light, real faces mid-moment, deep shadows with amber highlights. Groups over portraits.

LightWarm tungsten and stage light. Let highlights go amber.
ShadowDeep and warm. Crush blacks toward Kiln, not blue.
ColorSlightly lower saturation, a touch more contrast.
CropTight and wide. Leave dark space for type on one side.

+Do

  • Real people mid-moment: singing, laughing, praying, serving.
  • Shoot from the crowd and the side of the stage, not only the back.
  • Adults and students as the main subject. Kids only in groups.
  • Keep one dark side in the frame so headlines can sit there.
  • Ask before posting close faces. Keep a no-photo lanyard option.

×Avoid

  • Cold blue or green LED washes and heavy haze that hides faces.
  • Posed lineups, stock smiles and empty-room shots.
  • Single-child close-ups or readable name tags on kids.
  • Readable logos, other church names, alcohol or brands.
  • Neon glows, lens-flare filters and heavy presets.

07

Voice and tone

We sound like a friend who spots you across a loud room and waves you over. Short sentences. Plain words. Loud about Jesus, calm about everything else. No em dashes, no emojis.

Awake

Energy without hype. We sound like Sunday night, not a sales pitch.

Honest

We say the real thing, including what we are still figuring out.

Warm

Every line should feel like a hand on the shoulder.

Clear

When, where, what next. Always in the first few lines.

Brave

We invite people to big steps: baptism, serving, leading.

We say

  • The lamp is lit and the door is open. Come loud or come quiet.Welcome that sounds like a person.
  • Sundays 10 AM and 6 PM, 1180 Foundry Row.Times and place, plainly.
  • Bring your questions. Bring a friend.Short, two-beat invitations.
  • Here is what to expect.Take the guesswork out.
  • We would love to pray with you.Personal, never pushy.

We avoid

  • Church words like fellowship opportunities or corporate worship.
  • Hype like epic, insane or life-changing in every post.
  • Guilt and pressure: you should, do not miss out.
  • Body copy in all caps, em dashes and emojis.
  • Insider names for rooms and teams nobody new would know.
