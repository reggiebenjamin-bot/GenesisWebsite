Who we are

Nobody lights
a lamp to hide it.

We gather to shine together, then carry that light into every corner of Columbus.

Lead pastors

Andre and Talia

They started Lampstand in 2019 in the back room of a Short North coffee shop, with a handful of college friends and one guitar. Now a few hundred of us fill the Foundry every Sunday, and that back room feel never left.
