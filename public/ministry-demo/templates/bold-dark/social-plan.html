Lampstand Church: social marketing plan

Template preview. Lampstand Church is a made-up church in the Short North, Columbus, Ohio, built to show the Genesis Ministry Bold and Dark package. Swap in your own names, times, numbers and photos.

Who runs this: two people.

  • The media lead (staff or a committed volunteer, about 5 hours a week) plans the week, builds graphics in Canva, schedules posts, sends the email and answers DMs.
  • The Sunday helper (a volunteer, about 3 hours a week) shoots photos and phone video on Sunday, then cuts two short clips on Monday night.
  • A pastor reads each quote card before it goes out. That takes about ten minutes a week and is not a third job.

If a third person ever offers to help, give them the Sunday camera first. Everything below was planned for two.

Sundays: 10 AM and 6 PM at 1180 Foundry Row. The 6 PM gathering is live on YouTube.

2. Three goals, with numbers (December 2026 to March 2027)

  1. 15 Plan Your Visit sign-ups a month from social by March 2027 (from about 4 today), tracked with UTM links on every bio and story link.
  2. Reach 25,000 accounts a month in Columbus across Instagram and TikTok (from about 9,000), measured in each app's audience insights.
  3. 30 people take a next step through a social link by March 31: Baptism Sunday, a small group or a serve team.

6. The Sunday content workflow: one message, a week of posts

Thursday before: the preacher sends the media lead the big idea, three lines that might make good quotes, and the main scripture.

Sunday: the Sunday helper follows the shot list (section 10) at the 6 PM gathering, and records vertical phone video of one worship moment and the message from the side aisle. The media lead posts two or three live Stories from wherever they are sitting.

Sunday night (about 45 minutes): the media lead marks three clip moments with timestamps, posts the Sunday photo carousel, and checks that the YouTube live archive is up.

When the week falls apart (sickness, a holiday, a busy season), keep only these four: the Sunday photo carousel, clip 1, the Thursday invite and the email. That is about 2 hours, and nobody online will notice the rest is missing.

Batch once a month: on the first Monday, the media lead builds the month's four Thursday invites and one carousel template in Canva in a single sitting. That saves about an hour every week.

Rules for clips: start on the strongest sentence (no wind-up), captions on every clip in Instrument Sans Bold, the Lampstand mark small in a corner, and end on a plain call to action ("Full message at the link in bio").

10. Shot list for the Sunday helper

Shoot every scene both horizontal (website) and vertical (Stories and Reels). Leave dark space on one side of a few frames so headlines can sit there.

Before the gathering

  • Wide shot of people walking in through the main doors, greeters at the door
  • Two or three people talking over coffee in the lobby, cups in hand
  • Hugs and handshakes hello
  • Volunteers setting up: coffee, the tech booth, the welcome table
  • Details: the Lampstand flags, signage, string lights

Worship

  • Wide from the back with the stage light behind the crowd
  • Mid shot from the side aisle, faces lit by the stage
  • Two or three tight shots of adults or students singing, eyes closed or hands raised
  • Candid band shots: hands on keys, the drummer, the worship leader mid-song
  • Silhouettes of raised hands against the light

The message

  • The preacher mid-sentence, wide and tight
  • People listening from the side, Bibles and notes open

Prayer and response

  • Hands on shoulders at the front at the end (ask first, shoot from behind or the side)
  • Baptisms on Baptism Sunday: the moment out of the water and friends cheering

Kids

  • Check-in desk from behind the volunteers, groups of kids only
  • No single-child close-ups, no readable name tags, no faces of kids whose parents have said no

After

  • Lobby laughter, groups lingering, people praying together in corners

Settings that match the brand: warm white balance (3800 to 4500K), shutter 1/250 for moving hands, ISO up to 6400 rather than flash, f/1.8 to f/2.8. Avoid cold blue LED washes. Edit with the shared "Lampstand Warm" preset: lift the amber, deepen the shadows, pull saturation down slightly.

Consent: announce "photos today" from the stage once a month, offer no-photo lanyards at the welcome table, and delete any photo on request within a day.
