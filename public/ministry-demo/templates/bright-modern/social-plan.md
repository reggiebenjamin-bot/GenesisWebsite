# Lightwell Church: social marketing plan

**Template preview.** Lightwell Church is a fictional demo church in Raleigh, North Carolina, used to show the Genesis Ministry Bright & Modern package. Swap in your own church's names, times, numbers and photos.

Lightwell is a young church (planted 2023) with about 180 people across two Sunday gatherings, 9:00 and 10:45 AM, at 1218 Brightwater Ave in downtown Raleigh. Pastors Theo and Priya lead it. Most people found Lightwell because a friend invited them. This plan helps social media do two things: make it easy for members to invite friends, and make it easy for strangers to say yes.

The full plan suits a small team: one communications lead (staff or volunteer, about 4 to 5 hours a week), one volunteer photographer, one clip editor (about 2 hours a week), a Sunday story volunteer, and 15 minutes a week from the pastors.

### If your team is two people

Most church plants start here, and the plan still works. Two people split it like this:

| Person | Owns | Time a week |
|---|---|---|
| **Communications lead** (staff or a committed volunteer) | Scheduling the week, captions, quote cards and carousels from the Canva templates, the Thursday email, replies to comments and DMs, the monthly review | About 4 hours, most of it Monday afternoon |
| **Sunday volunteer** (photographer and clip maker) | Photos from the shot list, 5 live stories from the phone, 1 or 2 vertical clips in CapCut by Monday noon | About 4 hours, most of it on Sunday |

The pastors give 15 minutes on Sunday afternoon to mark one clip moment and one quote line in the shared doc.

**The two-person minimum week:**

- **Sunday:** 5 live stories.
- **Monday:** full message on YouTube, 1 clip to Reels, Shorts and TikTok.
- **Tuesday:** 1 quote card.
- **Wednesday:** 1 story poll.
- **Thursday:** The Lightwell weekly email, plus a People of Lightwell or Love Raleigh post.
- **Saturday:** the Sunday invite post and the Google Business Profile post.

That is 3 feed posts, 1 clip, 1 email and a handful of stories. Schedule all of it in one sitting on Monday. Add the carousel, the second clip and the TikTok test only after the basics have run for a month without missing a Sunday. In the 30-day calendar below, a two-person team keeps every Sunday, Monday, Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday row, turns each Wednesday row into a single story, and lets the Friday rows go when the week is full.

## 1. Who we are trying to reach

**New-to-Raleigh parents (main audience).** Couples aged 28 to 40 who moved for work in the Triangle, often with a toddler or a baby on the way. They live in or near Five Points, Oakwood, Boylan Heights, Mordecai and Glenwood South. They scroll Instagram at nap time, search "things to do in Raleigh with kids", and want to know one thing before they visit: will my kids be safe and will we feel awkward?

**The curious and the drifted-away.** Adults in their twenties and thirties who grew up around church, stepped away, and are open again but wary of pressure. They watch YouTube and short video, they read comments, and they want honest answers more than polish.

**Our own people, as messengers.** The 180 people already here. Every post should be something a member is glad to share or send to a friend in a DM.

What all three need to see quickly: when, where, what happens with the kids, and what it feels like inside.

## 3. Platform mix and why

| Platform | Role | Rhythm |
|---|---|---|
| **Instagram** | Home base. Young parents and young adults live here. Reels for reach, carousels for saves and shares, stories for Sunday energy and polls, DMs for real conversations. | 4 to 5 feed posts a week, stories most days |
| **Facebook** | Events and neighbors. Facebook Events for Christmas Eve, serve days and family nights. Grandparents, local parent groups and neighborhood groups (only where the group allows it). | Cross-post from Instagram through Meta Business Suite, plus every event |
| **YouTube** | The full message every Sunday, searchable for years. Shorts from the same clips. Titles written the way people search. | Full message by Monday 10 AM, 2 to 3 Shorts a week |
| **TikTok** | Yes, as an 8-week test. The look and the audience fit: a young church, honest teaching, real faces. Re-use the same vertical clips, so it costs almost nothing extra. Keep it if one clip a week reaches 1,000 local views or it brings in one visit a month. If not, stop and give the time to Instagram. | 2 to 3 clips a week, re-used from Reels |
| **Google Business Profile** | Not social, but it is where "church near me" searches land. Weekly post, fresh photos, and replies to every review. | 1 post and 5 photos a week |

## 4. Five content pillars

| Pillar | Share of posts | What it looks like |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Sunday Invite** | 25% | What to expect, times, parking, kids check-in, a 20-second "walk in with us" Reel, a "Your first Sunday" carousel. Always ends with Plan Your Visit. |
| **2. Message Moments** | 25% | Clips from Sunday with captions burned in, quote cards on cobalt, a "3 things from Sunday" carousel. |
| **3. People of Lightwell** | 20% | Short stories from members, volunteers and baptisms, told in their words, always with written permission. |
| **4. Love Raleigh** | 15% | Serve days, local partners, neighborhood shoutouts, favorite family spots around the city, Dix Park picnics. |
| **5. Midweek Help** | 15% | Prayer prompts, a dinner-table question for families, small group questions, a 60-second Q&A with Pastors Theo and Priya. |

## 6. The Sunday content workflow: one message, a week of posts

Example message: **"Light Comes, week 1: Hope for tired people"**, Pastor Priya, Sunday, December 6, 2026 (also Baptism Sunday).

| When | Who | What | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sunday during the gatherings | Volunteer photographer | Shoots the shot list (section 10). Uploads 30 to 50 keepers to the shared folder by 5 PM. | 3 hours |
| Sunday during the gatherings | Story volunteer | Posts 5 to 8 stories in real time. | 30 minutes |
| Sunday afternoon | Pastor Priya or the comms lead | Marks 3 clip moments with timestamps and 2 quotable lines in the shared doc. | 15 minutes |
| Monday by 10 AM | Clip editor | Uploads the full message to YouTube with chapters and a searchable title. Cuts 3 vertical clips, 30 to 60 seconds, captions burned in, in CapCut. | 2 hours |
| Monday | Comms lead | Builds 2 quote cards and 1 carousel from the Canva templates. Schedules the week in Meta Business Suite and TikTok. | 1.5 hours |
| Wednesday | Comms lead | Story poll from the message question. | 5 minutes |
| Thursday | Comms lead | Sends The Lightwell weekly. | 45 minutes |

**What that one message becomes:**

- **Clip 1 (Monday):** "Hope is not pretending you are fine." 45 seconds, Reel, Short and TikTok.
- **Clip 2 (Friday):** "You can be tired and still be held." 30 seconds.
- **Clip 3 (the next Monday, as a bonus):** the baptism moment from the same Sunday, shared with permission.
- **Quote card 1 (Tuesday):** "Hope is not a feeling you wait for. It is a person who comes to you."
- **Quote card 2 (Saturday story):** "You can be tired and still be held."
- **Carousel (Wednesday), 5 slides:** cover "3 things from Sunday" / 1. Hope starts with honesty / 2. Rest is not a reward, it is a gift / 3. Light shows up in the dark first / last slide: "Watch the full message" plus "Join us Sunday at 9:00 or 10:45".
- **Story poll (Wednesday):** "Heading into Christmas, how full is your tank?" Running on empty / Hanging in there.
- **Email (Thursday):** subject "Hope for tired people (and Christmas Eve times)". Three short paragraphs, the full message link, one quote card, one next step: invite a friend to Christmas Eve.

Short, warm, one clear next step. No emojis. Three to five local hashtags at most.

> Plan your visit at the link in our bio. We have been getting ready for you.
> #RaleighNC #DowntownRaleigh #RaleighFamilies

**2. Message clip**
> Hope is not pretending you are fine. Pastor Priya on what hope looks like when you are tired, from Sunday's message.
> Watch the full message on YouTube. Link in bio.

**3. Quote card**

**4. Carousel**

## 10. Shot list for the volunteer photographer

**Before you start:** daylight white balance, no flash, shoot wide and let people breathe in the frame. Keep whites clean and skin natural. Aim for 30 to 50 keepers, upload by 5 PM Sunday to the shared folder, and delete the rest.

**Arrivals and welcome**
- Wide of the front doors with greeters, from across the street
- Families walking in, from behind or the side (no close-ups of kids)
- Two adults hugging or laughing at the coffee bar
- Hands around coffee cups, the welcome table, the chairs before anyone arrives

**Worship and teaching**
- Wide from the back with hands raised, in good light
- Side angle of the congregation singing (faces of adults, mid-moment)
- Pastor teaching: one wide, one medium, framed so slide words are not readable
- Someone listening closely, notebook or Bible open

**People and moments**
- Small circles talking after the gathering
- Prayer: hands on shoulders, from a respectful distance
- Serve team in action (coffee, welcome, kids check-in hands and signs, no names)
- Baptism: the moment out of the water, and the crowd cheering

**The city**
- The building exterior on a sunny morning
- One Raleigh detail a week: the skyline, a Five Points corner, Dix Park

**Always**
- Ask before you photograph anyone up close. Respect anyone wearing a "no photos" lanyard.
- No children alone in a frame, no readable name tags on kids, no logos or brand names as the subject.
- Check the background for anything you would not want on a billboard.

## 11. What to measure every month

A 30-minute review on the first Tuesday of the month. Look at the numbers, then decide what to keep, stop and start.

| Metric | Where to find it | Month 3 target |
|---|---|---|
| Plan Your Visit forms from social | Website form source and UTM links | 12 a month |
| Accounts reached in Raleigh | Meta Business Suite, Insights | 15,000 a month |
| Saves and shares per post | Instagram post insights | 15 or more on average |
| Reel and Short average watch time | Instagram, YouTube Studio | Over 60% of the clip |
| Full message views and watch time | YouTube Studio | 150 views a week |
| Email signups from social, open rate | Mailchimp | 80 a month, 45% opens |
| Link in bio clicks | Link page analytics | 300 a month |
| Google profile searches, direction requests, reviews | Google Business Profile | 60 direction requests, 3 new reviews |
| DMs and comments answered within 24 hours | Instagram and Facebook inboxes | 100% |
| TikTok test (weeks 1 to 8) | TikTok analytics | One clip a week over 1,000 local views |

Followers matter less than visits. If a post type brings visits, make more of it.
