## Notes for whoever swaps in real photos

- pastor-teaching.jpg has faint projected slide words on the left wall. The page crops to the right of them; keep that crop if you reuse it.
- families-greeting-lobby.jpg has a few partial letters on the glass at top left. The homepage shows it in a tall frame cropped to the right of them; keep that crop or leave it out.
- friends-coffee-table.jpg has a book on the table whose cover lettering is legible at large sizes. It is only used at strip and social post sizes, where the lettering is unreadable. It is kept off the next-steps cards so the hero group does not appear twice near the top of the page. The hero uses friends-high-five.jpg instead.
- The set shares one grade: daylight, clean whites, cool shadows. Real church photos should be edited toward the same look (lift the shadows a little, keep whites white, no orange tint).
