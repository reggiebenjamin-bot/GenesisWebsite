The idea

A lightwell is an opening that lets daylight reach the rooms below.

That is the job of a young church in a busy city. The brand is bright, clear and confident, with one cobalt accent, lots of white space and real people in real daylight.

01

Logo

The mark is a lightwell seen in section. A shaft of light drops through the roof and lands as a floor, drawing an L. The small pane is the sky above. The wordmark is set lowercase in Bricolage Grotesque ExtraBold, tracked tight.

Minimum size

Lockup 96px wide on screens, 25mm in print. The mark alone can go down to 16px.

Color

Cobalt mark with ink type on light grounds, and with white type on night ink. The reversed mark with white type on cobalt and on photos. Never a cobalt mark on a blue ground.

Please do not

Stretch, rotate, outline, add shadows or glows, swap the font, or put the mark on a busy part of a photo.

04

Photography

Daylight, real moments and groups of adults doing ordinary church things together. Clean whites and cool, open shadows. Big crops with room to breathe, set in 8 to 10px corners.

The grade

Shoot or edit toward bright daylight. Lift shadows a little, keep whites white and skin natural. No orange tint, no heavy filters, no vignettes.

DaylightClean whitesCool shadowsTrue skin

Do

  • Groups of adults mid-conversation, mid-laugh, mid-song
  • Wide shots with real space around people
  • Kids in the background or in groups, never alone
  • Ask before you post anyone up close

Avoid

  • Stage-only shots with no congregation in them
  • Readable logos, brands or name tags
  • Single-child close-ups or children's faces with names
  • Blue overlays on faces, dark moody grades

07

Voice and tone

We sound like a friend who opens the blinds and says, come on in. Warm, plain and a little bit fun. We say what time things start and where to park before we say anything clever.

Bright

Hopeful and light on its feet. We smile in writing.

Honest

Real questions get real answers, including "we are not sure."

Welcoming

Written for the person who has never been to church.

Clear

Times, places and next steps first. One idea per sentence.

Unhurried

No pressure, no countdowns, no guilt. Come when you are ready.

Short sentences

If a sentence needs a breath, make it two.

No em dashes, no emojis

Use a period or a comma. Let the words carry the warmth.

Case

Buttons and menu names in Title Case. Headlines and links in sentence case.

Names

Pastors go by first names. Say "Pastor Priya," never "Senior Leadership."

08

Social posts

Three square posts and a story, built from the same parts as the website. Photo posts carry the times. Quote cards come straight from Sunday. Stories ask a question and point to one link.

How the posts are built

  • TypeBricolage Grotesque ExtraBold for the one big line, Hanken Grotesk for the rest. Never more than 12 words on a square post.
  • ColorWhite or cobalt grounds. On photos, a night ink fade from the bottom keeps white type above 4.5:1.
  • MarkAlways in a corner, never centered. The reversed mark on cobalt and on photos.
  • Safe zonesKeep words 120px from every edge of a story so the app's buttons never cover them.
  • PhotosDaylight, groups, real moments. Same grade as the website.
  • TemplatesRebuild these four as Canva templates so any volunteer can make a post in ten minutes.
