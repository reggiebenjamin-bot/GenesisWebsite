# Calder Hill Chapel: social marketing plan

**Template preview.** Calder Hill Chapel is a fictional church in Richmond, Virginia, used to show the Genesis Ministry Classic & Timeless package. A real church receives this plan rewritten around its own people, calendar and numbers.

This plan is built for a team of two, plus a pastor who gives it fifteen minutes a week.

| Who | Time a week | What they own |
|---|---|---|
| **Communications lead** (part-time staff or a committed volunteer) | About 5 hours | The calendar, Canva graphics, captions, scheduling, the Thursday letter, replies and the monthly numbers |
| **Sunday volunteer** (a member with a good phone or camera) | About 3 hours | Photos and stories on Sunday morning, the YouTube upload on Sunday afternoon, two short clips on Monday evening |
| **Pastor Samuel or Pastor Ruth** | 15 minutes | Three lines from the sermon on Monday morning; a read of the week's captions on Tuesday |

Everything below fits inside those eight hours. If a week falls apart, use the minimum week at the end of section 5.

**Before the first week (two evenings, once):** build five Canva templates from the brand board (Sunday invite, scripture or quote card, five-slide carousel, event post, story), set up the *Sunday Media* shared folder, and connect Instagram and Facebook in Meta Business Suite. After that, every graphic is a five-minute swap of words and photo.

---

## 1. Who we are trying to reach

| Who | What is true for them | What they need from us |
|---|---|---|
| **Neighbors within three miles** (the Fan, the Museum District, Carytown), mostly 25 to 45, many new to Richmond | They walk past the red brick building every week. They are curious about faith but wary of anything that feels like a sales pitch. | Proof that real, kind people are inside, plain answers about Sunday, and an easy first step. |
| **Adults returning to church**, 40 to 65, who grew up with hymns and drifted away | They miss something they cannot quite name. They want depth, honesty and a little quiet. | Sermons worth their time, a sense that they will not be judged, and a gentle invitation. |
| **Our own members and their adult children** | They love Calder Hill and would invite friends if we gave them something good to share. | Posts that are easy to forward, and news they hear first from us. |
| **Older and homebound members** | Facebook and YouTube are how they stay connected when they cannot come. | The full message every week, prayer requests, photos of familiar faces. |

## 2. Three goals for the next 90 days (through February 28, 2027)

1. **More first-time guests who found us online:** from about 2 a month to **6 a month**, counted from the "How did you hear about us?" line on the welcome card and the Plan Your Visit form.
2. **A bigger Thursday letter:** from 310 subscribers to **450**, with an average open rate of at least **55%**.
3. **More people hearing the message:** from about 60 to **150 YouTube views per Sunday message** within seven days of posting.

Each goal has one owner and one number we check on the first Monday of the month (see section 11).

## 3. Platform mix and why

| Platform | Role | How often | Why it fits Calder Hill |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Instagram** | Our front porch for neighbors: Reels, carousels, stories | 4 feed posts a week, stories most days | Where our 25 to 45 neighbors are. Short clips and carousels carry the Classic look well. |
| **Facebook** | The town square for members, families and grandparents | 3 to 4 posts a week, plus Events | Members already live here. Facebook Events are still the best way to invite people to Lessons and Carols and Christmas Eve. |
| **YouTube** | The library: every full message, plus Shorts | Full message every Sunday afternoon; 2 Shorts a week | Search lasts. A message posted today is still found next spring. Shorts reuse the same clips as Reels. |
| **Email (the Thursday letter)** | The most reliable channel we own | Every Thursday at 7:00 AM | People open it. It is ours, not an algorithm's. |
| **Google Business Profile** | The first thing people see when they search "church near me" | One update a week; reply to every review within 2 days | Free, local and often skipped by other churches. |
| **TikTok** | Not now | None | Our main audience and our small team are better served by Reels and Shorts, which use the same clips. Revisit in June if the youth team wants to own a TikTok with an adult leader approving posts. |

## 4. Five content pillars

1. **Sunday message.** The heart of the week, cut into pieces: clips, quote cards, a carousel, the full message on YouTube.
2. **Come and see.** Practical invitation: times, parking on Calder Lane, what to wear, what happens with kids, what Christmas Eve is like. Every week, one post answers one first-timer question.
3. **The family.** People, not programs: the choir, the kitchen crew, a longtime member's story, the greeters at the side door. Always with permission.
4. **Neighbors.** Calder Hill serving Richmond: Wednesday suppers, the food pantry, Christmas boxes, local partners.
5. **Roots and seasons.** 135 years on Calder Hill Road, the bell tower restoration, and the church year: Advent, Christmas, Lent, Easter, with the scripture and hymns that go with them.

A healthy month is roughly: 30% Sunday message, 25% Come and see, 20% The family, 15% Neighbors, 10% Roots and seasons.

## 5. Weekly rhythm

| Day | What goes out | Where | Who |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sunday** | 2 to 4 stories before and after services (never during prayer); full message uploaded to YouTube by 3:00 PM | IG and FB stories, YouTube | Sunday volunteer |
| **Monday** | Quote card from the sermon | IG and FB feed | Lead |
| **Tuesday** | Neighbors or Roots post; Google Business Profile update | IG, FB, Google | Lead |
| **Wednesday** | Clip 1 as a Reel and a Short; supper reminder in stories | IG, FB, YouTube Shorts | Lead schedules the volunteer's clip |
| **Thursday** | The Thursday letter at 7:00 AM; Sunday invite post | Email, IG and FB feed | Lead |
| **Friday** | Carousel (the message in five slides) or a Family post | IG and FB feed | Lead |
| **Saturday** | Story with a poll or question, then a "See you tomorrow" story with a Plan Your Visit link sticker | IG and FB stories | Lead (scheduled on Friday) |

Pastor Samuel or Pastor Ruth reads the week's captions on Tuesday morning. Everything from Monday to Saturday is scheduled ahead in Meta Business Suite, so nobody posts from a pew and nobody works on their day off.

**The minimum week.** When there is a funeral, a snow day or a sick child at home, do only three things: put the full message on YouTube, send the Thursday letter (four lines is fine), and post the Sunday invite on Thursday. Skip the rest without guilt and pick the plan back up next week.

## 6. The Sunday content workflow: one message, a week of posts

**Sunday (volunteer, about 90 minutes across the day)**
1. The sound desk records the message audio and a phone on a small tripod in the balcony records the 11:00 service.
2. By 3:00 PM the Sunday volunteer trims the message, adds a title in the format *Lift up your eyes | Psalm 121 | Pastor Ruth | Calder Hill Chapel*, chapters and a description with service times, and posts it to YouTube.
3. The volunteer drops the day's photos into the shared folder *Sunday Media / 2026-11-15* and stars the best 10.

**Monday (about 2 hours between the two of them)**
4. The pastor sends three lines from the sermon notes that could stand on their own.
5. In the evening the Sunday volunteer cuts **two clips** of 30 to 60 seconds in CapCut, with captions burned in, a 1-second title card in Newsreader and the arch logo at the end. One is a teaching moment, one is a warm or funny moment.
6. The lead builds in Canva from the brand board templates:
   - **One quote card** (parchment, double rule frame, Newsreader italic).
   - **One five-slide carousel:** 1. the big question, 2. the passage, 3. the main idea, 4. one thing to try this week, 5. "Join us Sunday at 9:00 or 11:00."
   - **One story poll**, for example "Which line from Psalm 121 do you need this week?" with two choices.
7. The lead drafts the **Thursday letter**: the message link, one photo, one upcoming event, one neighbor story, one prayer line.

**Tuesday to Saturday**
8. Schedule in Meta Business Suite: quote card Monday, clip 1 Wednesday, carousel Friday, poll Saturday, clip 2 as a YouTube Short on Friday.
9. Reply to every comment and message within a day, in the same warm voice. Invite, do not argue.

One 35-minute message becomes: 1 full YouTube video, 2 Reels, 2 Shorts, 1 quote card, 1 carousel, 1 story poll and a section of the Thursday letter.

## 8. Ten sample captions

Captions open with a plain, human line, say one true thing, and end with one clear next step. No emojis. Three to five hashtags on Instagram, none or one on Facebook.

**1. Sunday invite (Thursday)**
> This Sunday we light the second Advent candle and sing the old hymns that make December feel like December. Come as you are and sit wherever you like. The coffee is on in the parish hall from 8:30.
> Sundays at 9:00 and 11:00 AM, 1418 Calder Hill Road. Plan your visit at the link in our bio.
> #RVA #RichmondVA #RVAchurch #Advent

**2. Quote card (Monday)**
> "Help does not come from the hills. It comes from the One who made them." Pastor Ruth, on Psalm 121.
> If this is a hard week, tell us. Send a prayer request through the link in our bio, and our prayer team will pray for you by name.

**3. Sermon clip (Wednesday)**
> Pastor Samuel on waiting, and why the oldest songs of Advent are honest about how long it can take. Forty seconds, worth it.
> Watch the whole message on our YouTube channel, or come hear the next one on Sunday at 9:00 or 11:00.

**4. Giving Tuesday: the bell tower**
> Our bell has rung over Calder Hill Road since 1891. Last spring we found a crack in the frame that holds it, and it has been quiet ever since.
> The restoration costs $86,000. We have raised $52,400. Today, every gift up to $10,000 is matched by two families in our congregation. Help us ring it again by Easter. Give at the link in our bio.

**5. Lessons and Carols (event)**
> Nine short readings, carols old and new, candlelight and the 11:00 choir. Advent Lessons and Carols is one of our oldest traditions and one of the easiest ways to bring a friend to church.
> Sunday, December 13 at 5:00 PM. About an hour. Children welcome. Tap "Going" on our Facebook event so we know to set out extra chairs.

**6. Member spotlight**
> Evelyn has spent 60 Advents in the same pew, third from the front on the left. She taught Sunday school for 31 years and still makes the shortbread for the Lessons and Carols reception.
> "Every year somebody new sits next to me," she says. "That is my favorite part." Come sit next to Evelyn this Sunday.
> #RVAcommunity #CalderHillChapel

**7. First Christmas Eve carousel**
> Never been to a Christmas Eve service, or not for a long time? Here are six things to know before you come: where to park, which service is best with little ones, how long it lasts, what to wear, what happens with the candles, and who will meet you at the door.
> Christmas Eve at 5:00 and 9:00 PM. Swipe through, then share it with someone you would love to sit with.

**8. Wednesday supper**
> Every Wednesday at 6:00 PM, the long tables come out in the parish hall. Soup, bread, something sweet and good company. No sign-up, no cost, no speeches.
> Bring a friend, or come on your own and we will introduce you around. Suppers take a break after this week and return on January 6, so come hungry.

**9. Pantry volunteers**
> We are packing 300 Christmas boxes for families in our neighborhood this Saturday, and we need 40 more pairs of hands. Two hours, warm coffee, good music and a lot of canned green beans.
> Saturday, December 19, 9:00 to 11:00 AM in the parish hall. Sign up at the link in our bio. Teenagers welcome with a parent.
> #RVAvolunteer #RichmondVA

**10. Christmas Day**
> "Fear not: for, behold, I bring you good tidings of great joy, which shall be to all people." Luke 2:10
> From our family to yours, merry Christmas. Thank you to everyone who sang, served, packed, gave and came home to Calder Hill this season. Join us Sunday at 10:00 AM for one combined service.

## 10. Shot list for the Sunday volunteer

Shoot in natural light, no flash during worship, and leave space on one side of most frames for words. Take both horizontal and vertical versions of anything good.

**Every Sunday**
- The building from Calder Hill Road as people arrive (golden hour once a season).
- Greeters at the side door, mid-handshake or mid-hug.
- Two or three adults talking over coffee in the parish hall.
- A wide shot from the back or the balcony during the first hymn.
- The choir from the side aisle, singing, not posing.
- Hands on hymnals, hands at the communion rail, hands on shoulders during prayer.
- The preacher from the side aisle: one wide, one close, both with a natural expression.
- Heads bowed during prayer, taken from behind.
- The handshake line at the door after the 11:00.

**Monthly**
- Wednesday supper: the long table, the kitchen crew, the dessert table.
- Serving: pantry shelves, packing days, volunteers at work.
- Small details for texture: the bell rope, the stained glass in morning light, the worn pew ends, the brass plaques.
- The pastors outside, relaxed, for invitations and the website.

**Rules**
- Children only in the background or in groups, never a single-child close-up, never a readable name tag.
- Respect the "no photos" lanyard. When unsure, ask first.
- Never post a child's name. Ask adults before posting a close-up of them.
- Drop photos in *Sunday Media / date* by Sunday at 2:00 PM and star the best 10.
